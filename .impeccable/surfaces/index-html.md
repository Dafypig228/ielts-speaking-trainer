## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is the choice the student actually makes: which practice, for how much time. It is set as a departure board: four modes, duration in the time column, what is left for that mode in the status column. Exam day is the fixed departure everything counts down to. Refuses: stat tiles, goal rings, hero metric, card grid of actions.

OWN-WORLD: Kazakh railway station board. Deep board-blue header and tab bar, cool near-white ground, signal amber reserved for now / next (days left, selected tab, current mock stop, phrases due today), red for the record control, with the red header line and red dock only while live. Parts 1, 2, 3 are line colours (teal, orange, violet) on square route-number badges. Onest for everything (user's pick on 16 Nov, replacing Golos + PT Sans Narrow), bold for times, durations and countdowns. Tabular figures wherever numbers stack. Rows divided by 1px rules; shadows only on sheet and record dock.

STORY: Sees days left, picks a mode by the time he has and what is left, records, and finds the take plus coach findings directly under the question.
